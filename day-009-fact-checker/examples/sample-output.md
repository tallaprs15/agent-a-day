# Fact check
### Claim 1: The Great Wall of China is visible from space with the naked eye.
**Verdict: Refuted**
The claim that the Great Wall can be seen from space or the Moon with the naked eye is a persistent myth, as astronauts and NASA have confirmed it cannot be seen under normal conditions.
- [Artificial structures visible from space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_structures_visible_from_space)
  > "The claim that the Great Wall of China is the only man-made object visible from the Moon or outer space has been debunked many times"

### Claim 2: Mount Everest is the tallest mountain on Earth measured from base to peak.
**Verdict: Refuted**
Mount Everest is the highest mountain above sea level, but measured from its base to its peak, Mauna Kea is the tallest mountain on Earth.
- [Tallest mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallest_mountain)
  > "One way to do this is to measure the vertical distance from a mountain's base to its peak. On Earth, measured this way, Mauna Kea is the tallest mountain."

### Claim 3: Sydney is the capital of Australia.
**Verdict: Refuted**
Sydney is a major city in Australia, but the federal capital and seat of government is Canberra.
- [https://www.britannica.com/place/Australia](https://www.britannica.com/place/Australia)
  > "Its capital city is Canberra, and its most important economic and cultural centers are Sydney and Melbourne."
