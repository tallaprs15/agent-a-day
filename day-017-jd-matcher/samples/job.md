# Senior Data Platform Engineer, Northwind Metrics

Northwind Metrics builds analytics tooling for retail teams. We are hiring a senior engineer to own our data platform.

## What you will do
- Design and run batch and streaming data pipelines that serve customer dashboards.
- Improve reliability, testing and observability across the platform.
- Mentor engineers and help set technical direction.

## Requirements
- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience, mostly in Python.
- Strong SQL skills and experience with a cloud data warehouse such as Snowflake or BigQuery.
- Experience with workflow orchestration tools such as Airflow or Dagster.
- Hands-on experience with Kubernetes and AWS.
- Experience building streaming pipelines with Kafka or similar.

## Nice to have
- Experience with dbt.
- Experience with Terraform or other infrastructure as code.
- Prior experience in the retail or e-commerce domain.
